## Provenance and registry guardrails

Full text is per record, not per source family. Original LaTeX can be exact; PDF text/math recovery is not exact original LaTeX. IACR includes abstract fallbacks and some open-access twin recovery. Do not assert that every response has `corpus.as_of`.

Public MCP has 13 tools, including `get_research_bundle`. A bounded bundle is a read packet, not payment, a signed reading or confirmed prior art.

The policy in `api/registry_policy.py` requires decisive quorum and at least two distinct server-established trusted models for settled registry status. Caller filtering/provenance and deduplication are required. Wallet-only agreement is `read_once`; same-trusted-model agreement at quorum is `agreed_same_model`; opposing readings are `contested`. Client-declared names do not count. One signed wallet cannot itself yield `confirmed_prior_art`. Registry agreement is not scientific consensus.
