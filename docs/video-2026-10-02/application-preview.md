## Product description

dtox research helps Web3 builders gather evidence before choosing an execution-agent design. Through MCP, an agent can find primary research, retrieve original source formulas and inspect the security constraints stated by protocol authors. Source links and per-record extraction provenance stay attached to the results. Bounded reading packets limit context without substituting an invented conclusion for the source.

The preview demonstrates the MEV auction model in arXiv 2101.05511 and the ERC-4337 authors' EntryPoint security passage. A separate recorded discovery response identifies canonical Solana Durable Nonces documentation with full text. The preview is an editorial view of recorded responses, not continuous product footage.

Solana Attestation Service anchors signed, claim-specific readings. A wallet signature establishes authorship, not correctness or independence. Historical September 29 team-controlled Solana devnet records demonstrate attribution and separate x402 settlement. Base, Ethereum and Arbitrum have configured testnet support only. No new on-chain transaction or mainnet claim accompanies these videos.

The public research beta is free. Optional pay-per-research usage, without a subscription, team integrations and deeper evidence workflows are business proposals, not a claim of revenue, traction, exclusive paid tiers or external pilot feedback.
