What it returns

Method sections, not paraphrases

Where available, source text is split by structure: equations in raw LaTeX, algorithms, tables and prose are first-class chunks. Body availability is per record; some records retain only abstracts. Thirteen public MCP tools cover exploring by paper (find_papers, get_paper, read_paper_section, count_papers, similar_papers), by passage (search_research_paper, get_code_or_math_spec, compare_methods, research_trends), by bounded reading packet (get_research_bundle), and by claim (validate_project, get_verdict_message, record_signed_verdict).

Dense

Papers by title and abstract with bge-base-en-v1.5 (768 dim), and millions of structural chunks with bge-small-en-v1.5 (384 dim), both int8 ONNX in Qdrant.

Lexical

BM25 over papers and over chunks in SQLite FTS5, fused with the dense channels by reciprocal rank. Exact terms like GRPO survive here and drown in embeddings alone.

Citation graph

One hop through citation edges from retrieved seeds. The graph traversal is seed-dependent: changing query wording can change the retrieved seeds and therefore the graph results. Citation links add a recall channel, not wording invariance. Copies of one paper from different sources are merged into one result.

Verifiable verdicts on Solana

A retrieval score is not a verdict

search  ->  read
        ->  get_verdict_message
        ->  agent signs with its own Solana wallet
        ->  record_signed_verdict
        ->  Solana Attestation Service attestation (devnet)

A retrieval score measures wording, never whether a paper makes a claim. The public write path is wallet-signed: an agent signs its verdict with an ed25519 keypair it controls. A signature proves signer identity, not independent reasoning. Distinct wallets alone do not establish scientific quorum or confirmed_prior_art; wallet-only agreement remains pending. Scientific settlement requires decisive agreement across known trusted model identities established by the server. Recorded claims and explicit links can reuse readings, but arbitrary paraphrases are not guaranteed to resolve to the same claim.

The updated code settles paid signed writes upfront before the write handler; failures after settlement retain a receipt with no automatic refund. Paid reads use authorization and settle only after a successful handler. These changes have offline mocked SDK tests, not a newly deployed paid E2E verification.

Paid get_evidence_bundle calls the same get_research_bundle as the free read endpoint, with bounded limit, max_chars and strict arguments. Unsupported filters are rejected. Payment transport does not imply exclusive evidence or scientific validation.

What it does not do

Honesty as a feature

  • Four layers only: web3 (primary), AI agents, LLMs and small language models, and builder technology. An idea outside that scope is refused, not force-fit.
  • No patents. There are none in the corpus, and the service says that rather than letting absence read as novelty.
  • No guarantee that any given paper is present, inside the indexed layers or otherwise.
  • Full-text availability is per record, including IACR. Inspect evidence provenance and abstract-only fallback markers.
  • Empty results reflect index coverage and retrieval limits, never establish novelty; inspect evidence and scope.
  • Verdicts and attestations are devnet only.
