## Claim boundaries

- Empty results do not establish novelty. Patents are outside the indexed corpus.
- Formula fidelity is available only where the original source supports it. Recovered PDF extraction is not labeled exact original LaTeX.
- The comparison is about workflow scope. It does not assert that competitors lack features.
- The proposed paid-depth model remains a hypothesis. The public beta remains free.
- On-chain attribution proves authorship of a signed record, not factual correctness or reviewer independence.
