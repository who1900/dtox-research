## Claim guardrails

- Retrieval is evidence discovery, not a correctness, security, novelty, or safety verdict. No result does not prove absence.
- Corpus counters are dated inventory only. Web3 tags can overlap; records do not equal users; full-text availability varies by source.
- The one exact-LaTeX example is not a universal extraction guarantee. The associated section response had duplication/truncation; preserve that limitation.
- MCP, academic search, LaTeX retrieval, and citation graphs exist elsewhere. Describe the workflow and source scope without exclusivity claims.
- Solana signatures establish wallet attribution, not truth or independent reasoning. The separate historical payment and SAS examples are not one joined event.
- Three pilot teams and a 20-question competitor test are proposals, not actual pilots or measured results.
- Paid usage remains a business hypothesis. Keep the same research bundle free; do not imply paid access buys exclusive sources or better evidence quality.
