# Crypto World's Fair: submission checklist

Checked against public official sources on 2026-10-05. The signed-in registration/submission portal and the team's eligibility have not been inspected. This is a preparation checklist; it does not record a registration or submission.

## Official deadline and required materials

**Deadline: October 12, 2026, 23:59 Pacific (PDT, UTC-7) = October 13, 06:59 UTC = October 13, 11:59 Almaty (UTC+5).** Each entrant's registration and the leader's submission must be completed before the cutoff; Colosseum's clock controls. [Official rules, sections 5-6](https://colosseum.com/legal/Crypto%20World%27s%20Fair%20Hackathon%20Rules.pdf)

The official FAQ requires separate English videos: **pitch/presentation 2-3 minutes**, **product demo at most 3 minutes**, alongside a product name/description, integrated blockchains/tools, graphic, team backgrounds/location and GitHub link. Include go-to-market, demand-validation and distribution plans. Open source is encouraged; the FAQ permits a private repository with review access. dTox already has a public repository; no access change was made. [Fall 2026 hackathon and FAQ](https://colosseum.com/hackathon?year=fall2026)

Almaty has used UTC+5 since March 1, 2024. A host Windows/.NET timezone lookup returned the obsolete UTC+6 during verification; do not use its 12:59 conversion. [Government resolution No. 20](https://primeminister.kz/ru/decisions/19012024-20), [IANA Asia timezone rules](https://data.iana.org/time-zones/tzdb/asia)

## Disclosure and eligibility review

Prior work is permitted when disclosed; judging concerns contest-period work. Distinguish the team's pre-existing code from third-party open-source code and identify authorship/IP. The FAQ targets new startups without significant outside funding but supplies no numeric threshold. Obtain actual funding history. Solo founders may submit; each person may belong to one team/product. Every teammate must register and accept the rules. [Official FAQ](https://colosseum.com/hackathon?year=fall2026), [rules sections 3-7](https://colosseum.com/legal/Crypto%20World%27s%20Fair%20Hackathon%20Rules.pdf)

Eligibility includes age/local majority, residence/location and sanctions/prohibited-party restrictions, organizer/sponsor affiliation, employer and third-party obligations. Review section 3 in full instead of treating location alone as sufficient. All submitted content must be English and lawful, with necessary third-party and depicted-person permissions. Submission is not confidential. Third-party sponsorship and Colosseum trademark use may require advance written approval. [Official rules](https://colosseum.com/legal/Crypto%20World%27s%20Fair%20Hackathon%20Rules.pdf)

Formal judging considers functionality, impact, novelty, blockchain user experience, open source/composability and business plan. The FAQ also discusses founder-market fit, insight, execution, market, communication, viability and traction. For dTox, corpus counters are inventory and three pilot teams are a goal, so neither should be presented as existing traction. [Official FAQ](https://colosseum.com/hackathon?year=fall2026), [rules section 9](https://colosseum.com/legal/Crypto%20World%27s%20Fair%20Hackathon%20Rules.pdf)

## Final review before owner-authorized submission

- Two separate videos satisfy their individual duration limits and explain their evidence dates.
- Video URLs are accessible to judges; links are checked after an authorized upload.
- GitHub, deck, narration and submission describe the same selected version.
- Current claims distinguish the free read beta, proposed paid intensive usage, dated devnet evidence and pending EVM validation.
- Prior work, funding, roster, eligibility and final track are confirmed by the owner.
- The owner explicitly authorizes the public release/upload/submission actions.

Public-source review cannot establish these private account/team facts. No paid service, payment, signature, registry write, account change, public upload or submission is required to inspect the local package.
