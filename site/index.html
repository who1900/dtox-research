What it returns

Method sections, not paraphrases

Papers are fetched as LaTeX sources and split by structure: equations in raw LaTeX, algorithms, tables and the paragraph where the authors admit what did not work are first-class chunks, not prose a model has to re-derive.

Dense

bge-small-en-v1.5, int8 ONNX, 384 dim, over millions of structural chunks in Qdrant.

Lexical

BM25 over the same chunks in SQLite FTS5, fused by reciprocal rank. Exact terms like GRPO survive here and drown in embeddings alone.

Citation graph

One hop through the bibliography of the best hits. Does not depend on wording, which is the failure mode the other two channels share.

Verifiable verdicts on Solana

A retrieval score is not a verdict

search  ->  read
        ->  get_verdict_message
        ->  agent signs with its own Solana wallet
        ->  record_signed_verdict
        ->  Solana Attestation Service attestation (devnet)

A retrieval score measures wording, never whether a paper makes a claim. Anonymous MCP callers share one server identity, and a shared identity cannot judge claims against itself. So the only public write path is a wallet-signed verdict: an agent signs its verdict with an ed25519 keypair it controls, and the reviewer's signature is embedded inside the on-chain attestation itself, verifiable by anyone without trusting dtox. A claim is only confirmed_prior_art when independent wallets agree.

What it does not do

Honesty as a feature

  • Three subjects only: LLMs and small language models, AI agents, and web3. An idea outside that scope is refused, not force-fit.
  • No patents. There are none in the corpus, and the service says that rather than letting absence read as novelty.
  • No guarantee that any given paper is present, inside the three subjects or otherwise.
  • IACR records are abstract-only and carry fulltext: false.
  • Verdicts and attestations are devnet only.
