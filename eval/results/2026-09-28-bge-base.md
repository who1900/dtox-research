# Paper index on bge-base, 2026-09-28

Enabled in production: the paper-level index (title + abstract) re-embedded with bge-base-en-v1.5 (int8 ONNX, 768-dim) into papers_coarse_base, queries embedded by embed/embed_service.py on the Oracle host, and the dense paper channel weighted 2.0 in rank fusion (was 1.0). Chunk vectors stay on bge-small.

Weights were chosen on the dev split; numbers below are the held-out test split.

## Dev split: where the gain is (342 queries)

| configuration | hit@1 | hit@3 | MRR@10 |
|---|---|---|---|
| bge-small, dense paper channel only | 0.272 | 0.363 | 0.325 |
| bge-base, dense paper channel only | 0.290 | 0.377 | 0.342 |
| bge-small, full fusion (prod) | 0.269 | 0.363 | 0.329 |
| bge-base, full fusion, dense weight 1.0 | 0.272 | 0.366 | 0.331 |
| bge-base, full fusion, dense weight 1.5 | 0.272 | 0.371 | 0.333 |
| bge-base, full fusion, dense weight 2.0 | 0.275 | 0.374 | 0.334 |

The model is better on its own, but at the old weight the lexical and global channels drowned the difference. With bge-small the dense weight made no difference (1.0 / 1.5 / 2.0 all within one query), so the weight change is only worth making together with the new model.

## Hand-verified web3 set, test split (16 queries)

hit@3 0.438 -> 0.406, hit@10 0.562 -> 0.531, MRR 0.381 -> 0.372. One query each way: EIP-6963 (multiple injected wallet providers) fell from rank 2 to 4, and the Plasma query fell out of the top 10 (rank 10 before). Two others moved up by one.
