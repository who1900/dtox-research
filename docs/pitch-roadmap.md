# dtox research: pitch roadmap

This roadmap separates shipped research infrastructure from validation and deployment work. Dates are not promised; proposed business models are not traction.

## Built: research beta

- Web3-first corpus across papers, EIPs/ERCs, Solana SIMDs, and adjacent AI/LLM/builder topics.
- Structured retrieval for papers, sections, equations/code, citation links, and bounded research bundles.
- Public free MCP read interface and live research site.
- Provenance, abstract-only fallbacks, scope signals, and author-stated limitations surfaced for careful reading.
- Optional wallet-attributed verdict flow with historical Solana devnet SAS evidence; payment support is not represented as broadly live.

## Next: validate repeat use

1. Run an external builder/researcher pilot; measure repeat-use and gather evidence-quality feedback before making adoption claims.
2. Complete and verify paid EVM test-network flows end to end, with asset/facilitator support checked separately for each network.
3. Conduct a mainnet safety and operational review before any mainnet deployment or payment expansion.

## Longer-term questions

- Which workflows create durable value: protocol research, implementation planning, or prior-work discovery?
- What provenance and evaluation standard makes retrieved evidence useful without overstating novelty?
- Can attributed readings become reliably useful without confusing wallet authorship with independent scientific consensus?
- What pricing, if any, supports sustained operation? Pricing hypotheses are not current revenue.
