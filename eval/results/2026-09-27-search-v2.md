# Search v2, 2026-09-27

Enabled in production: Qdrant on the Oracle host, hierarchical retrieval (papers_coarse then chunks), paper-level BM25 over title+abstract (OR-matched), rank fusion of three channels with weights dense 1.0, paper-lexical 0.8, global chunk 1.0 (HIER_FUSION=rrf).

Weights were chosen on the dev split; numbers below are the held-out test split.

## Gates

- Golden harness: 16/17 before and after (onchain-oracle fails in both).
- Rank harness (canonical paper in top 3): 0/19 -> 4/19, MRR 0.016 -> 0.214.

## Found on the way

- fts_query ANDs every word: a sentence-long query matched no paper, so the paper-level channel was empty until it switched to OR.
- Rank fusion first under-weighted the global chunk channel and lost an audited case (entropy-kv-budget); raising that weight to 1.0 restored it.
- Latency grew by ~0.6s p50; the remaining cost sits in SQLite on the Contabo disk and in the extra channels.
