# Likely investor and developer questions

### Why would a protocol team use dtox instead of a general research assistant?

The wedge is a Web3-focused research corpus that joins papers with protocol specifications and returns source-level evidence such as equations and author-stated constraints where indexed. The pitch does not claim that general research tools lack API, MCP, or synthesis features.

### Is MCP a unique advantage?

No. MCP is an integration surface, not an exclusive moat. Elicit also offers API and MCP. dtox competes on its Web3 evidence workflow and corpus scope.

### What does the corpus size prove?

Only indexed inventory at the stated snapshot time. Counts do not establish customer demand, full-text availability, or adoption. Full text varies by record.

### What is the defensible asset?

The current hypothesis is a maintained Web3 corpus with structured source evidence and provenance. The recorded-reading registry is still small, so the deck does not claim a proven network effect. Reuse and feedback from repeated workflows remain to be validated.

### What has been validated commercially?

The public research beta is free. Optional credits or x402 access for heavier usage and team integrations are candidate models, not proven demand or a live exclusive paid tier. The next milestone is to recruit design partners and measure repeat use and willingness to pay.

### Does the auction equation apply to every MEV market?

No. It is Equation 4 from one paper's BEV auction model. The authors derive it under a simplifying common uniform reward distribution and prior knowledge of participant count and distribution.

### Is dtox an ERC-4337 security auditor?

No. It retrieves specification text that highlights EntryPoint risk concentration and the authors' formal-verification requirements. Builders must audit their own implementation.

### What does the Solana attestation prove?

That a wallet signed an attributable reading record. It does not prove that the reading is true, independent, or sybil-resistant. The demonstrated transaction is on devnet. No mainnet deployment is claimed.

### Why is the founder a fit for this problem?

The founder-provided CV reports solo product work across mobile, Web3, and AI since 2019, with blockchain, dApp, and wallet QA experience since 2020. It also reports two apps live in the Solana Seeker dApp Store. These are self-reported credentials, not independent user-traction metrics.

### Is this a fundraising ask?

The deck contains no raise amount or financing terms. It is ready for investor discussion without implying an unapproved ask.
